## SoundsUp

Let every live rhythm echo once more in the heartbeat of memory.  
#AdventureX2025

## About the Project

### Product Vision

SoundsUp is a new memory experience product that combines AI with portable hardware.

It works through a mobile app (and ideally a button-sized AI hardware device that can be attached magnetically or worn as a badge). The device automatically captures live sound, rhythm, and atmosphere in concerts, orchestras, livehouses, and other sound-centered entertainment settings.

AI analyzes the captured audio, identifies emotional expressions across different segments, and extracts highlight moments. It then generates a digital ticket stub that integrates event information, audio fragments, and emotional data—allowing each live performance to resonate again in a new dimension of time and memory.

### Product Features
The product has the following features:
1. Automatically captures live sound, rhythm, and atmosphere
2. AI-powered analysis of emotional expression types and highlight segments
3. Generates digital ticket stubs integrating event details, audio, and emotional data
4. Provides emotional analysis reports to help users better understand their own emotional responses

## Design notes

### Concert memories deserve more than a camera roll

The emotional texture of a live performance is difficult to preserve. Photos capture a scene, but often lose the sound, context, anticipation, and feeling that made the moment important.

SoundsUp reframes the ticket as a living memory object that grows from the traces people already create before, during, and after a show.

### A collection shaped by feeling

The app gathers lightweight signals, analyzes the emotional character of a memory, and generates a visual ticket that can be collected over time.

The result is not another social feed. It is a private archive of performances—organized around personal meaning rather than engagement metrics.
